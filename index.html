Hi, I'm Mona Saggu

Nutritionist helping families eat healthier without stress or waste.

With a background in Microbiology and Nutrition, I created homeonutri to make healthy eating simple, joyful, and sustainable.

No fad diets. No guilt. Just practical, science-backed swaps that save time, reduce waste, and make your family feel great.

Whether you're a busy parent, teacher, or community leader — I’m here to help you nourish better, every day.
